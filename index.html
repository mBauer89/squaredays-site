Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Square Days app ("the app") processes, why, and what rights you have. Square Days shows no ads, does not track you across apps or websites and does not sell your data.

What we process

  • Account data. When you sign in with Apple or with an email address, we receive a user ID and, depending on your choice, your email address or an Apple relay address. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and never stored by us in readable form.
  • Your habit data. The categories you create (name, colour, emoji, input type) and your daily entries, so they are saved and synced across your devices.
  • Push token. If you allow notifications, a device token is stored so we can send you reminders. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Purchases. Square Days Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription. Payment is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details; the app only receives from Apple whether your subscription is active.
  • Technical data. Basic technical data needed to connect securely to our backend, such as IP address and device type, processed by our service providers.

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not build profiles about you.

Why and on what legal basis

We process account and habit data to provide the app you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Push tokens are processed on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. Technical data is processed for secure operation of the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Service providers

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Cork, Ireland) under Apple's own privacy policy. We use Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) for authentication, data storage (Cloud Firestore) and push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and Apple for Sign in with Apple and notification delivery. Data may be processed on servers outside the EU, including in the USA. Such transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

We keep your data as long as you have an account. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and habit data within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data, and to object to processing. Write to info.squaredays@gmail.com. You may also complain to a supervisory authority, in Portugal the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD, www.cnpd.pt).

Children

Square Days is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always on this page.
